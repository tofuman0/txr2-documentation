### Recovered progression conditions

Before the first stage-boss defeat, the source selector requires type −4 leaders in source categories 9, 10 and 11 to be defeated and at least 26 defeated team leaders across those categories. The selector also considers the active car class, previous encounters, undefeated candidates and whether a rival is already present. These are source selection rules; live presentation and geographic area names require separate verification.
