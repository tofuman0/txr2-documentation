### Recovered progression conditions

Before the first stage-boss defeat, the source selector requires at least eight defeated team leaders in source category 10 and a defeated type −3 leader in the same category. The selector also considers the active car class, previous encounters, undefeated candidates and whether a rival is already present. These are source selection rules; live presentation and geographic area names require separate verification.
