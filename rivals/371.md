## Challenge response

This rival’s source record enables a challenge-response check at classification value 13. In the following-special-actor path, if the player’s car category differs from the rival’s category and the [saved driving-classification value]({{ "/driving-classification.html" | relative_url }}) is below 13, an approach that remains in state 7 for at least 180 source ticks can set the cancellation flag. A matching car category or a classification value at or above the threshold bypasses this particular check; other approach and encounter conditions still apply.

The original normal-car records matching source category 64 are: [R30M (ID 115)]({{ "/cars/115.html" | relative_url }}), [R30 (ID 116)]({{ "/cars/116.html" | relative_url }}).

These values follow the US source-recovered `084C0E` response branch and the car category byte at CarInfo `0x05`. They describe this branch, not an unconditional guarantee that the challenge will start.
