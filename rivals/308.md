### Progression requirements

Before defeating the first stage boss, defeat at least eight leaders from [this group’s teams]({{ "/quest-boss-progression.html#group-9" | relative_url }}) and an early group leader from the same group. This can make the rival eligible for selection. Selection also depends on your car class and other encounter conditions.
