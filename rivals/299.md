These factors affect the automatic driver’s target speed under the specific following conditions described on [Game Details]({{ "/game-details.html" | relative_url }}); they are not car-power multipliers. The signed distance is the source relation multiplied by 0.3125. At either exact threshold the distance rule leaves the target unchanged. The additional flag-gated factor can combine with the distance factor. Battle reward multipliers are separate.
