Before defeating the first stage boss, defeat the early zone bosses in groups 9 and 10 and at least 21 team leaders across groups 9–11. See the [teams and bosses in those groups]({{ "/quest-boss-progression.html" | relative_url }}). This can make the rival eligible for selection. Selection also depends on your car class and other encounter conditions.
