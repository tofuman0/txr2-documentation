### Recovered progression conditions

Before the first stage-boss defeat, the source selector requires type −4 leaders in source categories 9 and 10 to be defeated and at least 21 defeated team leaders across source categories 9–11. The selector also considers the active car class, previous encounters, undefeated candidates and whether a rival is already present. These are source selection rules; live presentation and geographic area names require separate verification.
