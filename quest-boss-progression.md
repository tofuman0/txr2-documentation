# Quest boss progression

Quest tracks team leaders and bosses in five groups. The names below show which teams and bosses belong to each group. Defeating a team leader counts for that group; meeting a threshold makes a boss eligible for selection, but its appearance and challenge still depend on the game’s other rules. The game may select a boss variant according to car class and previous encounters.

## First stage

For groups 9 and 10, the recovered selector opens the early group-leader selection after four team-leader defeats in that group. Its zone-boss selection requires eight team-leader defeats in that group and a win over an early group leader.

The group 11 zone-boss selection requires the group 9 and 10 zone bosses to be defeated and at least 21 team-leader defeats across groups 9–11. After a zone boss in each of groups 9, 10 and 11 has been defeated and the total reaches 26 team leaders, the first stage-boss selection can become eligible: [Purple Comet]({{ "/rivals/321.html" | relative_url }}) or [White Phantom]({{ "/rivals/322.html" | relative_url }}). These are group gates; they do not require defeating every listed variant in a single Quest period.

## After the first stage boss

For groups 9, 10, 12 and 13, the recovered selector requires two team-leader defeats in the group for its advanced group leader; its zone boss requires four team-leader defeats and the group-leader win. Group 11’s advanced zone boss requires a team-leader defeat in that group. After the five advanced zone groups have each been completed, the selector can admit the final rival, [???]({{ "/rivals/323.html" | relative_url }}).

The game also considers the current Quest period, active car class, prior encounters, undefeated candidates and whether a rival is already present. These rules have source and seeded-test support, not an end-to-end verified natural playthrough.
