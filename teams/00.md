## Appearance

This team uses [appearance pool 0]({{ "/rival-appearance-pools.html#pool-0" | relative_url }}). The pool includes roads that also contain the Kasumigaseki and Shibakouen starting points; individual rivals may be placed elsewhere.

This team is included in the source’s regular-team roster before the current Quest period records a defeat of the first stage boss. Pool activation and selection still determine whether a member appears during an outing.

The [leader]({{ "/rivals/000.html" | relative_url }}) can enter the candidate pool when not already defeated or present and either previously encountered or all other team members have been defeated.
