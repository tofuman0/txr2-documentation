# Rival priority and parts unlocks

Some parts become available when a defeated rival meets a priority threshold. The game checks the highest priority among the defeats it is currently using. For a requirement of 9 or higher, for example, any rival in groups 9–15 meets that part of the rule. The rival must still appear and accept a challenge, and the shop may have other unlock or compatibility conditions.

The game uses either current-period or retained defeat records depending on Quest state. Some parts also have separate persistent unlock conditions. These priority values come from the recovered US rival records; they are internal progression values, not a rating of driving skill.
