# The Port

The Dreamcast is one of my favourite consoles, and Tokyo Xtreme Racer and Tokyo Xtreme Racer 2 are two of my favourite games on it. I've always been a fan of Japanese cars, and I own an AE86 myself. Over the years, I kept coming back to TXR2.

## Shutokou Battle Online

I discovered Shutokou Battle Online (SBOL) while its service was still live, but it was only available in Japan, so I never managed to play it at the time. The idea of playing a TXR game on PC always appealed to me.

I'd been programming from a young age and got into reverse engineering in 2010, working on other MMO projects. Around 2018 or 2019, I rediscovered SBOL and decided to reverse engineer it and create a private server to bring the game back to life.

By 2019, I had a server with many aspects of the game working. After a Japanese gaming website picked it up, it reached a peak of around 500 active users. I've since shut the server down and changed the game so it can now be played offline.

## Tokyo Xtreme Racer Zero

TXR0 interested me because it came out around the same time as SBOL, and a lot of what I'd learned carried across. I used that knowledge to create editors for TXR0. Even so, I wasn't as happy with the way TXR0 felt compared with TXR2.

## Why port TXR2?

TXR2 is the game I kept returning to. With my experience of the series, the work I'd done on SBOL and TXR0, and the help of AI, a native Windows port felt much more achievable. That's why I started this project.

The port is an unofficial fan project. For downloads and setup information, visit the [distribution page](https://github.com/tofuman0/TXR2-Port-Distribution).
